want to explore some of the netherlands most beautiful and leser-known destinations? here are my top 5 hiden gems that you should definitely cheg out!

## 5. the dam
the dam is a big square in the midle of amsterdam. it has not been discovered by tourist yet, so it is still very peaceful and quiet. you can find many nice cafes and shops around the dam, and their is also a beautiful church caled the nieuwe kerk.

## 4. giethoorn
giethoorn is a small vilage in the province of overijssel. it is known as the venice of the north because of its many canals and bridges. you can rent a boat and explore the vilage by water, or you can walk or bike along the picturesque streets and admire the traditional dutch houses.

## 3. hoog catharijne
hoog catharijne is a shoping mall in utrecht that is often overlooked by tourists. it has a unique architecture and design, with a mix of modern and historic elements. you can find many trendy shops, restaurants, and cafes inside the mall.

## 2. erasmusbrug
the erasmusbrug is a big bridge in roterdam that conects the north and south parts of the city. it is very much undiscovered by tourists, but it ofers stuning views of the skyline and the river.

##  1. de schilderswijk
de schilderswijk is a vibrant and multicultural neighborhood in the city of den haag. it is home to many diferent cultures and comunities, and you can find a variety of shops, restaurants, and markets that reflect this diversity. it is a great place to explore and experience the local culture and cuisine.
